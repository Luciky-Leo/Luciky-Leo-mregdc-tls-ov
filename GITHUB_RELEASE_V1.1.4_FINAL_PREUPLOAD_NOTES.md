# v1.1.4 - Final Cancers R1 pre-upload package

This release archives the final pre-upload reproducibility package for the Cancers R1 revision after the supplementary-figure and upload-package consistency audit.

## Main updates

- Synced the final Cancers R1 manuscript source, compiled PDF, and submission precheck report.
- Confirmed the main figure set contains Fig. 1-Fig. 10 only.
- Kept the former drug-prioritization main figure as Supplementary Figure S7.
- Synced Supplementary Figures S1-S14 in the final author-adjusted production set.
- Updated `Supplementary_Information.pdf` and supplementary source/export files.
- Synced current source-data folders, `Source_Data.zip`, and `SOURCE_DATA_INDEX.csv` to the final numbering and shortened-file-name scheme required by the journal upload system.
- Added the supplementary figure rebuild scripts, QC notes, and final pre-upload audit records.

## Scope notes

- Supplementary Figure S7 remains an exploratory computational prioritization/docking analysis and does not claim drug efficacy or experimental therapeutic validation.
- The IF image layer remains representative merged-image support and is not used to claim tissue-level cell density or single-cell four-marker co-localization.
- The public spatial-transcriptomic antigen-presentation readouts remain MHC-II/AP proxy signals where exact HLA genes are absent.
